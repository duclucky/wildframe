# React Bits

Upstream: https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits
Commit: 310fc737fadcaa149a4e06fb088fc95332dc8bfb

WildFrame adapts the pointer-centred radial light and masked border from
`src/content/Components/SpotlightCard/SpotlightCard.jsx` and `SpotlightCard.css`,
and the once-only fade, scroll-container trigger and stagger behaviour from
`src/content/Animations/FadeContent/FadeContent.jsx`.

The native DOM/CSS adaptations live in `source/visuals.js` and
`source/index.template.html`. They are part of this website. React and GSAP
are not bundled. These adaptations are not offered as a component library.
